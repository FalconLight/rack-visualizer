PDF and SVG are drawn to scale in millimetres, and keep each part on its own layer (racks, rack units, devices, data ports, power ports, one layer per cable type, labels), so CAD programs such as AutoCAD (PDFIMPORT), Illustrator or Inkscape can switch them on and off.
